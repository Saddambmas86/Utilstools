# Design System Inspired by WordsCompare

> Category: Technology & SaaS
> A confident comparison-tools identity built from a vivid red W mark, warm orange accents, and precise midnight typography.

## Color Palette

- Primary red `#FF2D55` is the signature brand color for the logo and key actions.
- Orange `#FF8A50` adds warm energy to secondary emphasis and the W mark.
- Purple `#7B61FF` identifies success and calculator-oriented category accents.
- Blue `#2563EB` supports informational actions and document-tool categories.
- Midnight `#0F172A` anchors text and dark navigation.
- Light background `#F1F5F9`, pure white surfaces, and pale blue-gray `#E7EDF5` borders create a clear working canvas.
- Use red-orange gradients only for the core identity and selected, high-priority brand moments.

## Typography

Use a geometric, modern sans-serif led by `"Inter", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`. Set the Words wordmark in midnight and Compare in vivid red. Use strong compact headings, readable regular body text, and consistent medium-to-bold controls. Technical values use a clear monospace stack.

## Voice & Tone

Write direct, optimistic utility copy. The tagline is “Compare. Calculate. Create.” Explain tools and results clearly, keep labels short, and avoid unnecessary promotional language.

## Imagery & Iconography

The signature logo is a bold white W formed from two interlocking document-like strokes over a red-to-orange rounded square. Keep it legible at favicon scale. Use consistent simple icons for categories: purple calculators, blue PDF, teal text, orange developer tools, red business tools, and violet image tools. Avoid gradients outside the identity mark and a few focused accents.

## Layout & Spacing

Use a light `#F1F5F9` canvas, white cards, midnight text, and restrained cool borders. Keep the existing compact responsive workspaces and 4px-based spacing; use consistent 8–16px radii. Primary actions use red with white text, secondary actions use outlined or pale surfaces, and focus states use a visible red-tinted ring. Preserve 44px touch targets on mobile.

## Agent Prompt Guide

Apply the reference WordsCompare identity faithfully: red-orange W logo, midnight “Words”, red “Compare”, and the tagline “Compare. Calculate. Create.” Align application surfaces to the light blue-gray canvas, navy text, white cards, and category-specific icon colors. Use red for primary actions, orange sparingly for warmth, blue for information, purple for success/calculator identity, and teal for text tools. Keep layouts compact, consistent, accessible, and responsive.
